Everything you need to know about BloomPlayer.

The basics
BloomPlayer is a web app for children to watch YouTube videos without any player controls — no rewind, no skip, no progress bar. A parent sets it up, locks it with a PIN, and hands it to the child. The video simply plays forward.
It was built by a mom of a teenager on the autism spectrum. The rewind button became a loop he couldn't escape—the same second of video, repeated for hours. BloomPlayer removes the controls entirely to prevent this habit from forming.

Sign-in & Account
Yes. Signing in creates a secure cloud profile to hold your personalized library and Parent PIN. It ensures your settings are saved safely and follow you across devices.
We only store your email to identify your account, your saved video library, your child's age group selection, and your PIN. Your child never creates an account, and we never sell your data to anyone.
Parent PIN & Security
You create your 4-digit PIN during the initial app setup. To change it, long-press the BloomPlayer logo on the home screen to open the Settings menu.
On the PIN entry pad, tap Send reset link. We'll send a secure link to your email address that allows you to reset your PIN.
BloomPlayer is a habit-prevention tool, not a high-security lock. We highly recommend using Guided Access (iOS) or App Pinning (Android) to physically lock your child into the app.
Videos & Watching
Yes! Use the "My Library" tab to paste any YouTube link. It will save to your secure list, and your child will only ever see the videos you have manually approved.
If you find a video that isn't working or isn't appropriate, please email us at hello@bloomplayer.com. We personally review these reports to keep the library safe for everyone.
