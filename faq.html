Everything you need to know about BloomPlayer.

The basics
BloomPlayer is a web app for children to watch YouTube videos without any player controls — no rewind, no skip, no progress bar. A parent sets it up, locks it with a PIN, and hands it to the child. The video plays forward.
Built by a mom to help prevent "stimming" behavior in children with autism, where the rewind button reinforces repetitive loops. BloomPlayer removes the controls entirely.

Parent PIN & Security
You create your 4-digit PIN during setup. To change it, long-press the BloomPlayer logo to enter Settings.
Tap "Send reset link" on the PIN screen. We'll email you a secure link to reset it.
For older children, we highly recommend using Guided Access (iOS) or App Pinning (Android) to lock them into the app.
